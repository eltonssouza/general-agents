# LGPD: processing by Public Authorities (Poder Público) (Art. 23 to 32)

Pages = PDF (10-12). Labels: `[from source]`, `[inference]`, `[extrapolation]`, `[DATED]`. Not legal advice; refer to a lawyer/encarregado (DPO). This file is brief: the collection covers public authorities in a short chapter.

## Rules (Section I)
- `[from source]` Legal entities under public law (those in the sole paragraph of Art. 1 of the Access to Information Law, LAI) process data to fulfill their public purpose, in pursuit of the public interest, to carry out legal competences or public-service duties, provided that: (I) they inform the cases of processing, with legal basis, purpose, procedures and practices, in easily accessible outlets, preferably on websites (LGPD Art. 23, I, p.10); and (III) they appoint an encarregado (LGPD Art. 23, III, p.11; wording of Law 13.853/2019). Items II and IV appear as vetoed (LGPD Art. 23, II and IV, p.11).
- `[from source]` The ANPD may rule on the forms of publicity (§1º); the law does not exempt authorities from the Access to Information Law (§2º); deadlines and procedures for rights follow specific legislation (Habeas Data, Administrative Procedure, LAI) (§3º); notary and registry services receive the same treatment (§4º) and provide electronic access to the administration (§5º) (LGPD Art. 23, §§1º-5º, p.11).
- `[from source]` Public companies and mixed-capital companies operating in competition (Art. 173 of the Constitution) are treated as private-law legal entities; when operationalizing public policies, as the bodies and entities of Public Authorities (LGPD Art. 24, p.11).
- `[from source]` Data kept in an interoperable and structured format for shared use, for public policies, public services, decentralization and access to information (LGPD Art. 25, p.11).
- `[from source]` Shared use serves specific purposes of public policies and legal attribution, respecting the principles of Art. 6º (LGPD Art. 26, caput, p.11). It is prohibited to transfer to private entities data from databases to which it has access, except: decentralized execution of public activity (I); publicly accessible data (III); legal provision or contracts and agreements (IV, added in 2019); or exclusively fraud and irregularity prevention or protection of the data subject's security and integrity (V, added in 2019) (LGPD Art. 26, §1º, p.11). Item II appears as vetoed. Contracts and agreements are communicated to the ANPD (LGPD Art. 26, §2º, p.11).
- `[from source]` Communication or shared use from a public-law person to a private one is reported to the ANPD and depends on consent, except for the consent exemption provided in the law, the publicity of Art. 23, I, or the exceptions of Art. 26, §1º; the reporting to the ANPD will be regulated (LGPD Art. 27, p.12; sole paragraph added in 2019).
- `[from source]` Art. 28 vetoed (LGPD Art. 28, p.12). The ANPD may request at any time processing operations and information on the scope and nature of the data, and issue a complementary technical opinion (LGPD Art. 29, p.12; wording of Law 13.853/2019) and establish complementary rules for communication and shared use (LGPD Art. 30, p.12).

## Liability (Section II)
- `[from source]` In case of infringement by public bodies, the ANPD may send a notice with measures to stop the violation (LGPD Art. 31, p.12). It may request Public Authority agents to publish a RIPD and suggest standards and good practices (LGPD Art. 32, p.12).
- `[from source]` The sanctions applicable to public bodies are those in items I, IV, V, VI, X, XI and XII of Art. 52, without prejudice to other laws (LGPD Art. 52, §3º, p.18).

## Connections
- Legal bases for public authorities: Art. 7º, III (p.4) and Art. 11, II, b (p.6). International transfer with publicity: Art. 33, VII (p.13).
- `[inference]` For government systems, three engineering requirements follow from the text: publication of the cases of processing (Art. 23, I), interoperability and data structure (Art. 25), and an inventory of sharing contracts reportable to the ANPD (Art. 26 §2º).
- `[extrapolation]` ANPD regulations on sharing within public authorities and sector guidance are not in the collection.
