# Fidelity report: design-patterns-expert (v2, post-audit)

This report replaces the v1 one, which only validated page ranges and declared "19 blind tests". It contains: the inventory of the sources, what the audit found in the v1 package, what was fixed, the verifier results, the coverage and what was **not** done. Pages = PDF (see `references/INDEX.md`).

## 1. Source inventory and page convention
| Code | File | Real nature | Pages | Printed pagination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RG | `Design-Patterns-RefactoringGuru.pdf` | capture of the refactoring.guru **website** (22 patterns; p.1 is a "404"); it is **not** the e-book "Dive Into Design Patterns" | 279 | none |
| GPP | `Game-Programming-Patterns-Nystrom.pdf` | Nystrom's book as a web capture; **Subclass Sandbox chapter absent** (p.190 is a 404) | 338 | none |
| CC | `Clean-Code-SampleChapter-Pearson.pdf` | **sample**: Introduction, **Ch. 1**, table of contents, index; **Chs. 2 and 3 missing** | 67 | body: PDF − 30 |
| PEAA | `PoEAA-SampleChapter-Pearson.pdf` | **sample**: Introduction, **Ch. 3**, table of contents, index; **Chs. 1, 2 and 9 missing** | 78 | Intro: PDF − 25; Ch. 3: PDF − 7 |

v1 did not declare a page convention and mixed two: `patterns_index.json` (auto-generated by keywords) used PDF pages, while the prose used invented numbers (in RG, offsets of about +20 pages, e.g. "Command p.192" when Command is p.172) and, for CC and PEAA, **printed pages of the complete book** (of chapters that are not in the PDFs). v2 standardizes on the **PDF page** and provides the map in `INDEX.md`.

The texts were extracted with PyMuPDF 1.27 to `scratch/audit/design-patterns-expert/src/{RG,GPP,CC,PEAA}.txt` with `=== PAGE N ===` markers.

## 2. Findings in the v1 package (before the fixes)
### 2.1 Automatic layer (extraction of all "(SIGLA p.N)" citations)
- **304 citations** in 20 `.md`/`.py` files of v1 (GPP 152, RG 71, CC 49, PEAA 32).
- **3 outside the document range** (for example "RG p.45-285", but the PDF has 279 pages).
- Of the **109** citations whose stretch names an identifiable pattern/topic, only **23 (21%)** point to a page where the name appears; **86 (79%) point to a page where the pattern does not appear** (failures by source, including the 3 out of range: RG 44 of 71 citations, GPP 30 of 152, PEAA 9 of 32, CC 6 of 49). The other 192 citations carry no identifiable name (not automatically assessable). The 23 coherent ones include many coincidences (e.g. the name "Adapter" appears in navigation links).
- `patterns_index.json` (131 KB, "44 concepts") was generated by word matching, with truncated excerpts and no gap labels.

### 2.2 Manual layer (reading the cited pages): 80 claims from v1
Classification: **OK 2 (2.5%)**, **wrong page 30 (37.5%)**, **overstatement 12 (15%)**, **unsupported 36 (45%)**. By source: RG 19 WP, 7 NS, 2 OS; GPP 10 WP, 13 NS, 8 OS, 1 OK; CC 10 NS, 1 OK; PEAA 6 NS, 1 WP; package 2 OS. Full table in section 7. Relevant examples of unsupported or distorted claims:
1. **Clean Code rules (Chs. 2-3)** presented as `[from source] (CC p.17-50)`: "fewer than 20 lines", "more than 3 arguments is forbidden", "flag arguments forbidden", CQS, `I` prefix on interfaces, and even a translated quote in quotation marks. **The chapters are not in the sample**; only the table of contents and index.
2. **PoEAA**: "three main layers (PEAA p.18-24)", Transaction Script with a "trap", Table Module tied to .NET 1.1, Service Layer and a "Martin Fowler Decision Matrix (PEAA p.48-52)". Chs. 1, 2 and 9 are **not in the PDF**; the cited pages (PDF) fall in the Introduction and Ch. 3, which deal with other subjects.
3. **Subclass Sandbox (GPP p.198-202)** with `Superpower`, `move`, `playSound`: the chapter is **absent** from the PDF (p.190 is a 404 page).
4. **Invented quote**: a block in quotation marks attributed to Nystrom about the Lapsed Listener, plus "recommends Weak References" (not stated; GPP recommends unregistering, p.55-57).
5. **Invented numbers**: a 1000 x 1000 tile map with a 500 KB texture in Flyweight, GC pauses of 100 to 300 ms, 500 projectiles in Object Pool, "cap of 5 to 8 updates" in Game Loop, "reduces from O(N²) to O(N)" in Spatial Partition, "2 shapes x 3 colors = 6 classes" in Bridge (RG shows 2 x 2 = 4).
6. **Distortions**: Dirty Flag "propagates the flag to the children" (the book's code does the opposite: it passes a parameter when rendering, p.302-303); Prototype "recommended" for spawning (Nystrom says he found no case where it was the best answer, p.64-67); Component "adopted by Unity, Unreal and Godot" (GPP cites Unity, Delta3D and XNA, p.228); an interpolation formula that is not the book's (p.140-141).
7. **Nature of the sources**: RG treated as the e-book "Dive Into Design Patterns" and "23 patterns" (the PDF is the website, with 22).
8. **Structure**: the agent prompt had 41 lines and ended at "Use of Deterministic Tools" (truncated: no reference lookup, response format, boundary, `[DATED]` content); the README had no installation instructions; `AGENTS.md` and the Copilot agent were missing; there were no test scripts.
9. **Scripts**: `clean_code_function_auditor.py` and `domain_logic_selector.py` encoded unsupported rules (numeric limits and a "Service Layer recommendation" with PEAA pages); `game_loop_simulator.py` fixed a cap of 8 and "death spiral" as if they were from the book.

## 4. Verifier results (v2)
- `tests/verify_citations.py --src <folder>`: **198 positive checks** (RG 54, GPP 93, CC 21, PEAA 30) and **25 negative** (plausible claims that the PDFs do not contain): **0 failures**. On the first run there were 4 failures, all by the audit author himself (3 shifted page ranges in the checks and 1 negative that revealed that "VBLANK" appears in passing at GPP p.155); they were fixed, including a sentence in `padroes-de-sequenciamento-e-tempo.md`.
- `tests/audit_tokens.py --src <folder>`: **106 backticked terms** checked against the cited page: **0 failures, 0 neighbors**; and **850 page citations** from every `.md` of the package with a valid range (none outside 1-279, 1-338, 1-67, 1-78). On the first run it found 2 real slips (`Breed::newMonster` and `update(elapsed)` were not literal on the pages), already fixed.
- Spot numeric check (audit script, outside the package): 205 numeric tokens from the cited excerpts; the 36 not found were reviewed by hand and are list numbering, PDF page totals, printed index pages and numbers written out in words in the PDFs ("fourteen", "twelve"); no errors.
- Automatic name-on-page audit applied to the v2 package: **702 citations**, 0 out of range, 254 coherent, 14 flagged, all reviewed and explained (pattern name cited on another page of the same chapter; "404" page; and 2 that are the report's own citations to wrong v1 pages); 434 with no identifiable name.
- `tests/run_script_tests.py --src <folder>`: **27 cases + 2 verifiers**, 0 failures (the English edition of the package adds CT-26, a Portuguese-input lookup, for 28 cases). Scripts actually run: `patterns_lookup.py` (search, `--list`, `--topic`, gaps, NOT COVERED with exit 1), `game_loop_simulator.py` (`--demo`, `--frames`, `--render-pos`, `--check-step`), `domain_logic_selector.py` (`--logic`, `--inheritance`, `--complexity`, `--demo`), `clean_code_function_auditor.py` (`--signature`, `--checklist`, `--demo`).

## 5. Audit coverage (what was and was not read)
- **Read in full and used for rewriting:** RG p.2-279 (except advertisements); GPP p.1-338 (all chapters present); CC p.7-9 (table of contents), 25-27 (introduction), 31-45 (Ch. 1) and index entries by keyword (p.47-65); PEAA p.8-17 (table of contents), 26-38 (Introduction), 40-60 (Ch. 3).
- **Not read or only skimmed:** prefaces and acknowledgments of CC (p.19-23) and PEAA (p.18-25); the complete PEAA index (p.62-78) and the CC index outside the keywords; code texts (pseudocode/C++) in the PDFs were read for the plot, **not** verified line by line; images and diagrams were not analyzed (extraction yields only text); the "+/-" pros-and-cons icon of RG is lost in extraction, so the polarity of each item is by reading.
- **Limits of the automatic verification:** `verify_citations.py` and `audit_tokens.py` prove the presence of terms on the cited pages, **not** the full meaning of the sentence; hence the manual reading. The manual sample of 80 claims was made on **v1** (where the errors were); v2 was written directly from reading the pages and verified by the automatic layers and by the author's own review.
- **There was no blind execution by an independent model**: no other model generated answers to the test cases without seeing the package; the cases (`casos-de-teste.md`) verify that the content for the correct answer exists in the prompt, the references and the scripts. There was also no review by a second human reviewer.
