# LGPD: administrative sanctions, enforcement, ANPD and Council (Art. 52 to 59)

## Sanctions (Art. 52)
- `[from source]` Applicable by the ANPD to infringements of the rules of the law (LGPD Art. 52, caput, p.17):
  - I warning, with a deadline for corrective measures;
  - II simple fine of up to 2% of the revenue of the private-law legal entity, group or conglomerate in Brazil in the last fiscal year, excluding taxes, limited to R$ 50,000,000.00 per infringement;
  - III daily fine, subject to the limit of item II;
  - IV publicizing of the infringement after it has been investigated and confirmed;
  - V blocking of the data to which the infringement refers until regularization;
  - VI deletion of the data to which the infringement refers (LGPD Art. 52, I-VI, p.17).
- `[from source]` VII to IX vetoed; X partial suspension of the operation of the database for up to 6 months, extendable for an equal period; XI suspension of the exercise of the processing activity for up to 6 months, extendable; XII partial or total prohibition of activities related to processing (X to XII added by Law 13.853/2019) (LGPD Art. 52, VII-XII, p.17-18).
- `[from source]` Sanctions are applied after an administrative procedure with full defense, gradually, singly or cumulatively, as the case may be, considering: seriousness and nature of the infringements and of the rights affected; good faith; advantage gained or intended; economic condition; recidivism; degree of damage; cooperation; repeated and demonstrated adoption of internal mechanisms capable of minimizing the damage; adoption of a good practices and governance policy; prompt adoption of corrective measures; proportionality (LGPD Art. 52, §1º, I-XI, p.18).
- `[from source]` Other points: does not replace sanctions of the Consumer Defense Code and specific legislation (§2º); items I, IV, V, VI, X, XI and XII may reach public entities and bodies (§3º); in calculating the fine the ANPD may consider the total revenue of the company or group if it lacks the revenue of the line of business or if it is incomplete (§4º); the proceeds go to the Fund for the Defense of Diffuse Rights (§5º); sanctions X, XI and XII come only after at least one of those in items II to VI in the same case (§6º); individual leaks and unauthorized accesses under Art. 46 may be the subject of direct conciliation, and without agreement the penalties apply (§7º) (LGPD Art. 52, §§2º-7º, p.18).
- `[from source]` The ANPD defines by its own regulation, submitted to public consultation, the methodologies for calculating the base amount of fines, previously published, with objective calculation (dosimetria) and reasoning (LGPD Art. 53, p.18-19). The daily fine considers the seriousness and extent of the damage, is reasoned, and the notice states the obligation, the reasonable deadline and the amount (LGPD Art. 54, p.19).
- `[from source]` Effective date: Arts. 52, 53 and 54 entered into force on 1 August 2021 (LGPD Art. 65, I-A, p.24; item added by Law 14.010/2020).
- `[inference]` Use for risk prioritization: the text gives the spectrum and the calculation criteria. Criteria VIII, IX and X (internal mechanisms, governance policy, corrective measures) are the ones the organization controls: keep dated evidence of them. ANPD calculation regulation and amounts applied: `[extrapolation]` and `[DATED]`.

## ANPD (Chap. IX)
- `[from source]` The ANPD is an autarchy of special nature linked to the Ministry of Justice and Public Security, with functional, technical, decision-making, administrative and financial autonomy, headquartered in the Federal District (LGPD Art. 55-A, p.19; wording given by Law 15.352/2026 according to the compiled text). §§1º to 3º of Art. 55-A and Art. 55-B appear as revoked by Law 14.460/2022 (LGPD Art. 55-A, §§1º-3º, p.19).
- `[from source]` Composition: Board of Directors, National Council for the Protection of Personal Data and Privacy, Inspector General's Office (Corregedoria), Ombudsman, Attorney's Office, Audit and administrative and specialized units (LGPD Art. 55-C, p.19; items V-A, V-B and VI as per Law 15.352/2026). The Board of Directors has 5 directors, a 4-year term, chosen by the President and approved by the Senate (LGPD Art. 55-D, p.19-20).
- `[from source]` Competences (summary): ensure the protection of data and of trade and industrial secrets; oversee and sanction with adversarial proceedings and full defense; consider petitions from data subjects after a complaint not resolved by the controller; promote knowledge and studies; encourage standards that facilitate control by the data subject; international cooperation; rule on the publicity of operations; request a notice from the public authorities (LGPD Art. 55-J, I-XII, p.20-21).
- `[from source]` Also: issue regulations and procedures, including on RIPD in high-risk cases; hear agents and society; carry out or order audits, including in the public authorities; enter into a commitment to eliminate irregularity; issue simplified rules for microenterprises, small companies and startups; ensure clear information to the elderly; decide with finality on the interpretation of the law and unforeseen cases; coordinate with sector regulators; simplified mechanisms for recording complaints (LGPD Art. 55-J, XIII-XXIV, p.21).
- `[from source]` Minimum intervention when imposing conditions on private agents; regulations preceded by public consultation and hearing and regulatory impact analysis; coordination with sector regulators; complaints may be analyzed in aggregate form (LGPD Art. 55-J, §§1º-6º, p.22). The application of sanctions is exclusively the ANPD's competence, the central body for interpreting the law (LGPD Art. 55-K, p.22).
- `[from source]` National Council: 23 representatives of Executive and Legislative bodies, CNJ, CNMP, CGI.br, civil society, scientific institutions, union confederations, business and labor sectors (LGPD Art. 58-A, p.23); proposes strategic guidelines, annual reports, studies and debates, and disseminates knowledge (LGPD Art. 58-B, p.23-24). Arts. 55, 56, 57, 58 and 59 appear as vetoed (LGPD Art. 55, p.19; Art. 58, p.23).
- `[extrapolation]` ANPD resolutions and guides (incident notification, calculation of fines, encarregado, international transfer, small agents) are not in the collection; see `extrapolacoes-e-datado.md`.
