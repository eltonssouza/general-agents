# LGPD <-> NIST IR 8062 mapping (privacy engineering objectives)

Generated by `python -I scripts/map_lgpd_nist.py markdown` from `scripts/lgpd_nist_mapping.json` (the terms of each row were checked against the cited pages by `tests/verify_citations.py`). Pages = PDF. Support material; not legal advice.

## How to read (honesty rule)
- **Neither source cites the other.** NIST IR 8062 is from 2017, for US federal agencies; the LGPD is Brazilian law. The correspondence is an engineering comparison made by the agent.
- **TEXTUAL** level: (a) NIST explicitly links the concept to the objective (Figure 8 in NIST8062 p.26, sections 3.1.1.x in NIST8062 p.26-28 or the glossary in NIST8062 p.36) and (b) the LGPD text defines the same concept with equivalent terms on the cited pages. Even so, present it as a "comparison", not as a legal rule.
- **INFERENCE** level: conceptual link without direct textual support; label it `[inference]`.
- **NO_SUPPORT** level: LGPD topic with no equivalent in NIST IR 8062 (for example legal bases, children, international transfer, sanctions, ANPD).
- The NIST P/M/D objectives do not replace the LGPD principles (Art. 6º, p.3-4) or legal analysis; they serve to turn principles into verifiable system capabilities.

## Summary by objective (TEXTUAL rows only)
- **Predictability**: purpose (Art. 6º I), suitability/context (Art. 6º II), transparency (Art. 6º VI; Art. 9º), accountability (Art. 6º X).
- **Manageability**: necessity (Art. 6º III), free access (Art. 6º IV), quality (Art. 6º V), correction/deletion (Art. 18 III, IV, VI), consent (Art. 7º I; Art. 8º), accountability.
- **Disassociability**: necessity (minimization), anonymization (Art. 5º XI; Art. 12), pseudonymization (Art. 13 §4º), accountability.
- Outside the three objectives: security (NIST links it to CIA), non-discrimination (problem category), RIPD x PIA, probability and severity x likelihood and impact, life cycle (Art. 46 §2º).

## NIST concepts with no LGPD counterpart

- Problematic data action as a risk factor (NIST p.29): The LGPD does not use this concept; the RIPD (Art. 5º, XVII) speaks of processes that may generate risks to civil liberties and fundamental rights [inference by approximation].
- Disassociability as an engineering objective (NIST p.28): The LGPD has anonymization/pseudonymization and necessity, but no equivalent system objective.
- Assessing effectiveness (notice read and understood) rather than the existence of the notice (NIST p.22,26): The LGPD asks for clear and conspicuous information (Art. 9), without requiring a comprehension test.
