# LGPD x NIST IR 8062 glossary (terms, equivalents and where they are)

Pages = PDF. The Brazilian terms follow the LGPD (compiled text); the English terms follow NIST IR 8062. Equivalences marked `[inference]` are the agent's: the sources do not cite each other. Support material; not legal advice.

| Term (pt-BR, LGPD) | Closest term (NIST IR 8062) | Short definition and where |
|---|---|---|
| dado pessoal (personal data) | PII (personally identifiable information) | LGPD: information related to an identified or identifiable natural person (LGPD Art. 5º, I, p.2). NIST: information that distinguishes or traces identity, alone or combined with other linkable information (NIST8062 p.36). Equivalence `[inference]`: both broad |
| dado pessoal sensível (sensitive personal data) | (no equivalent of its own) | categories of LGPD Art. 5º, II (p.2); NIST gives only the example of the health context (NIST8062 p.31) |
| dado anonimizado / anonimização (anonymized data / anonymization) | de-identification, anonymity (taxonomy cited) | LGPD Art. 5º, III and XI (p.2-3); NIST cites the possible taxonomy (NIST8062 p.28) |
| pseudonimização (pseudonymization) | pseudonymity | LGPD Art. 13, §4º (p.7); NIST lists the term (NIST8062 p.28) |
| titular (data subject) | individual | LGPD Art. 5º, V (p.2); NIST uses "individuals" for those who suffer the problem (NIST8062 p.17) |
| controlador, operador, agentes de tratamento (controller, processor, processing agents) | (agency / system owner; no exact equivalent) | LGPD Art. 5º, VI, VII, IX (p.2-3); NIST speaks of agencies, system owners and engineers (NIST8062 p.12) |
| encarregado (data protection officer, DPO) | Senior Agency Official for Privacy (partial analogue) | LGPD Art. 5º, VIII (p.3) and Art. 41 (p.14); NIST glossary (NIST8062 p.36). Roles are not equivalent |
| tratamento (processing) | processing; data actions | LGPD Art. 5º, X (p.3); NIST: operations on PII, across the full lifecycle (NIST8062 p.36; NIST8062 p.31) |
| consentimento (consent) | individual participation (FIPP, in the consent part) | LGPD Art. 5º, XII (p.3); NIST App. F (NIST8062 p.49) |
| finalidade; adequação (purpose; suitability) | purpose specification and use limitation | LGPD Art. 6º, I-II (p.3); NIST App. F (NIST8062 p.49) |
| necessidade (necessity) | minimization | LGPD Art. 6º, III (p.3); NIST App. F (NIST8062 p.49) |
| livre acesso (free access) | access and amendment | LGPD Art. 6º, IV (p.3); NIST (NIST8062 p.49) |
| qualidade dos dados (data quality) | quality and integrity | LGPD Art. 6º, V (p.4); NIST (NIST8062 p.49) |
| transparência (transparency) | transparency | LGPD Art. 6º, VI (p.4); NIST (NIST8062 p.49) |
| segurança (security) | security (confidentiality, integrity, availability) | LGPD Art. 6º, VII (p.4); NIST p.49 and Fig. 8 (NIST8062 p.26) |
| responsabilização e prestação de contas (accountability) | accountability | LGPD Art. 6º, X (p.4); NIST (NIST8062 p.49) |
| relatório de impacto (RIPD) (data protection impact report) | PIA (privacy impact assessment) (functional analogue) | LGPD Art. 5º, XVII (p.3), Art. 38 (p.14); NIST (NIST8062 p.23) |
| incidente de segurança (security incident) | (NIST IR 8062 does not address incidents) | LGPD Art. 48 (p.16); term "incident" absent from NIST |
| programa de governança em privacidade (privacy governance program) | privacy program (A-130); privacy engineering | LGPD Art. 50, §2º, I (p.16-17); NIST describes engineering and risk management (NIST8062 p.6; NIST8062 p.21) |
| autoridade nacional (ANPD) (national authority) | (no equivalent; US context: OMB, agencies) | LGPD Art. 5º, XIX (p.3) and Art. 55-A (p.19) |
| transferência internacional (international transfer) | (no equivalent) | LGPD Art. 5º, XV (p.3) and Art. 33 (p.12-13) |
| — | privacy engineering | systems engineering discipline focused on avoiding problems for individuals when processing PII (NIST8062 p.18-19; NIST8062 p.36) |
| — | predictability | reliable assumptions about PII and its processing (NIST8062 p.25; NIST8062 p.36) |
| — | manageability | granular administration of PII: alteration, removal, selective disclosure (NIST8062 p.25; NIST8062 p.36) |
| — | disassociability | processing without association to individuals beyond what is operationally necessary (NIST8062 p.25; NIST8062 p.36) |
| — | problematic data action | data action that causes an adverse effect for individuals (NIST8062 p.29) |
| — | privacy problem | adverse experience due to the processing of PII (NIST8062 p.17) |
| — | context | circumstances of the processing of PII (NIST8062 p.31) |
| — | system privacy requirement | system requirement with privacy relevance, verifiable (NIST8062 p.37) |
| — | privacy control | administrative, technical and physical safeguards (NIST8062 p.36) |
| — | FIPPs | fair information practice principles of OMB A-130, listed in NIST8062 p.49 |
| — | trustworthiness | deserving trust in meeting critical requirements (NIST8062 p.37) |
| — | byproduct risk model | privacy risk arising from authorized processing (NIST8062 p.16) |
| — | system boundary | system boundary; can be widened for privacy (NIST8062 p.10; NIST8062 p.19) |
| bloqueio; eliminação (blocking; deletion) | (no equivalent of its own) | LGPD Art. 5º, XIII-XIV (p.3); NIST cites discarding obsolete data as a manageability capability (NIST8062 p.27) |
| uso compartilhado (shared use) | disclosure/transfer (data actions) | LGPD Art. 5º, XVI (p.3); NIST (NIST8062 p.31) |
| legítimo interesse (legitimate interest) | (no equivalent) | LGPD Art. 7º, IX (p.4) and Art. 10 (p.6) |
