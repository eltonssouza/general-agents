# LGPD: final provisions, effective date and amendment notes of the compiled text (Art. 60 to 65)

Pages = PDF (1-25). Labels: `[from source]`, `[inference]`, `[extrapolation]`, `[DATED]`. Not legal advice; refer to a lawyer/encarregado (DPO).

## Final provisions
- `[from source]` Amends the Marco Civil da Internet (Brazilian Internet Civil Framework): Art. 7º, X (definitive deletion of personal data provided to an internet application, on request, at the end of the relationship, except for mandatory record keeping) and Art. 16, II (keeping of excessive data relative to the consented purpose is prohibited) (LGPD Art. 60, p.24).
- `[from source]` A foreign company is notified and summoned of all acts through the agent or representative of a branch, agency, subsidiary or office in Brazil (LGPD Art. 61, p.24). The ANPD and Inep issue regulations for access to education data (LGPD Art. 62, p.24). The ANPD establishes rules for progressive adaptation of databases constituted up to the law's entry into force (LGPD Art. 63, p.24). The rights and principles of the law do not exclude others from the legal order or from treaties (LGPD Art. 64, p.24).

## Effective date (Art. 65, wording of Law 13.853/2019)
- `[from source]` I: on 28 December 2018, arts. 55-A to 55-L, 58-A and 58-B (the text's list includes 55-B, now revoked). I-A: on 1 August 2021, arts. 52, 53 and 54 (item added by Law 14.010/2020). II: the remaining articles 24 months after publication (LGPD Art. 65, I, I-A and II, p.24). The law is dated 14 August 2018 (LGPD Art. 65, p.24).
- `[from source]` The PDF states that the text does not replace the one published in the DOU (Diário Oficial da União, official gazette) of 15.8.2018 (LGPD footer, p.25).
