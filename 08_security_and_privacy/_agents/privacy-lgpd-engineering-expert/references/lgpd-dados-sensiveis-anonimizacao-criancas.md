# LGPD: sensitive data, anonymization, research, children and end of processing (Art. 11 to 16)

Pages = PDF (6-8). Labels: `[from source]`, `[inference]`, `[extrapolation]`, `[DATED]`. Not legal advice; refer to a lawyer/encarregado (DPO).

## Sensitive data (Art. 11)
- `[from source]` May only be processed: (I) with the consent of the data subject or legal guardian, in a specific and highlighted manner, for specific purposes; or (II) without consent when indispensable for: a) legal or regulatory obligation; b) shared processing for public policies by the administration; c) studies by a research body, with anonymization whenever possible; d) regular exercise of rights (including contract and proceedings); e) protection of life or physical safety; f) health protection (wording of Law 13.853/2019); g) fraud prevention and data subject security in identification and authentication processes of registration in electronic systems, safeguarding the rights of Art. 9º and except where the data subject's rights and freedoms prevail (LGPD Art. 11, I-II, p.6).
- `[inference]` Reading the list literally, Art. 11 does not repeat legitimate interest or the credit protection of Art. 7º, IX and X (p.4). This affects, for example, biometrics for authentication (see item g) and profiling with health data. The interpretation is a legal matter: take it to a lawyer.
- `[from source]` The rule applies to any processing that reveals sensitive data and may cause harm to the data subject, subject to specific legislation (LGPD Art. 11, §1º, p.7).
- `[from source]` Exemption from consent by public bodies in items a and b requires publicity (LGPD Art. 11, §2º, p.7). Communication or shared use of sensitive data for economic advantage may be prohibited or regulated by the ANPD (LGPD Art. 11, §3º, p.7).
- `[from source]` Health data: communication or shared use between controllers for economic advantage is prohibited, except for the provision of health services, pharmaceutical and health care, for portability at the data subject's request, or financial and administrative transactions of the services (LGPD Art. 11, §4º, p.7; wording and items from Law 13.853/2019). Private health plan operators may not use health data for risk selection in contracting or in excluding beneficiaries (LGPD Art. 11, §5º, p.7).

## Anonymization and pseudonymization (Art. 12-13)
- `[from source]` Anonymized data is not personal data, unless the process is reversed with the controller's own means or can be reversed with reasonable efforts; reasonableness considers cost, time, available technologies and exclusive use of own means (LGPD Art. 12, caput and §1º, p.7).
- `[from source]` Data used to form the behavioral profile of an identified natural person may be considered personal (LGPD Art. 12, §2º, p.7). The ANPD may rule on anonymization standards and techniques and verify their security, after hearing the Council (LGPD Art. 12, §3º, p.7).
- `[from source]` Pseudonymization: the data loses the possibility of association with an individual except through the use of additional information kept separately by the controller in a controlled and secure environment (LGPD Art. 13, §4º, p.7). It appears in the law in the context of public health studies by research bodies (LGPD Art. 13, caput, p.7).
- `[from source]` Public health studies: access to databases by research bodies, processing only within the body and for the purpose of the study, in a controlled and secure environment, with anonymization or pseudonymization whenever possible and ethical standards (LGPD Art. 13, caput, p.7); results may not reveal personal data (§1º); the body answers for security and may not transfer to a third party (§2º); regulation by the ANPD and health authorities (§3º) (LGPD Art. 13, §§1º-3º, p.7).
- `[inference]` In engineering: "anonymized" is a claim to be tested (who has the means to reverse it? at what cost?), not a label. Conceptual connection with NIST disassociability: see `mapeamento-lgpd-nist.md` (M12, M13).
- `[extrapolation]` Specific techniques (k-anonymity, differential privacy, tokenization) are not addressed by either source; NIST IR 8062 only cites a possible taxonomy and the zero-knowledge proof as an example (NIST8062 p.28).

## Children and adolescents (Art. 14)
- `[from source]` Processing in their best interest; for children, specific and highlighted consent from at least one parent or legal guardian (LGPD Art. 14, caput and §1º, p.8). Controllers keep public the information on types of data, use and exercise of rights (LGPD Art. 14, §2º, p.8).
- `[from source]` Collection without consent only to contact the parents (single use, no storage) or for the child's protection, and never passed on to a third party without consent (LGPD Art. 14, §3º, p.8). Do not condition games and applications on data beyond what is strictly necessary (LGPD Art. 14, §4º, p.8). Reasonable efforts to verify who consented, with available technologies (LGPD Art. 14, §5º, p.8). Simple, clear and accessible information, with audiovisual resources when appropriate (LGPD Art. 14, §6º, p.8).
- `[inference]` The text speaks of "crianças" (children) in §1º and "crianças e adolescentes" (children and adolescents) in the caput; the age definition is not in the compiled text (it comes from another law: `[extrapolation]`, confirm with a lawyer). Later rules on the children's digital environment are `[extrapolation]` and `[DATED]`: see `extrapolacoes-e-datado.md`.

## End of processing and deletion (Art. 15-16)
- `[from source]` Processing ends by: purpose achieved or data no longer necessary; end of the period; communication from the data subject (including revocation, safeguarding the public interest); or ANPD order in case of violation (LGPD Art. 15, I-IV, p.8).
- `[from source]` After the end, data is deleted within the technical limits of the activities, with retention authorized for: legal or regulatory obligation; study by a research body (anonymization whenever possible); transfer to a third party; exclusive use by the controller, without third-party access and with anonymized data (LGPD Art. 16, I-IV, p.8).
- `[inference]` Backups, logs and replicas are "storage": deletion needs a policy covering them, with the caveat of the "technical limits" of the text; document exceptions and deadlines. The text does not fix retention periods (term "prazo de retenção" (retention period) absent).
