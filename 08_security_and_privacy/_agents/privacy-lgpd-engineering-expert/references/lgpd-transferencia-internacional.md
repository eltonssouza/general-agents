# LGPD: international data transfer (Art. 33 to 36)

Pages = PDF (12-13). Labels: `[from source]`, `[inference]`, `[extrapolation]`, `[DATED]`. Not legal advice; refer to a lawyer/encarregado (DPO).

## Definition
- `[from source]` International transfer = transfer of personal data to a foreign country or an international body of which the country is a member (LGPD Art. 5º, XV, p.3).

## Permitted cases (Art. 33): "only permitted" in the cases
| Inciso (item) | Case | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| I | country or body with a degree of protection adequate to that provided in the law | (LGPD Art. 33, I, p.12) |
| II | controller offers and proves safeguards in the form of: a) specific contractual clauses; b) standard contractual clauses; c) global corporate rules; d) regularly issued seals, certificates and codes of conduct | (LGPD Art. 33, II, p.12) |
| III | international legal cooperation between public intelligence, investigation and prosecution bodies | (LGPD Art. 33, III, p.12) |
| IV | protection of the life or physical safety of the data subject or a third party | (LGPD Art. 33, IV, p.12) |
| V | ANPD authorization | (LGPD Art. 33, V, p.12) |
| VI | commitment made in an international cooperation agreement | (LGPD Art. 33, VI, p.13) |
| VII | execution of public policy or legal attribution of the public service, with publicity (Art. 23, I) | (LGPD Art. 33, VII, p.13) |
| VIII | specific and highlighted consent, with prior information on the international nature of the operation, distinguishing it from other purposes | (LGPD Art. 33, VIII, p.13) |
| IX | necessity for the cases of Art. 7º, II (legal obligation), V (contract) and VI (exercise of rights) | (LGPD Art. 33, IX, p.13) |

- `[from source]` Public-law persons may request the ANPD to assess the level of protection afforded by a country or body (LGPD Art. 33, sole paragraph, p.13).

## Role of the ANPD
- `[from source]` The ANPD assesses the level of protection considering: general and sector rules of the destination; nature of the data; observance of principles and rights; security measures provided in regulation; judicial and institutional guarantees; other circumstances (LGPD Art. 34, I-VI, p.13).
- `[from source]` The ANPD defines the content of the standard clauses and verifies specific clauses, global corporate rules and seals, certificates and codes of conduct (LGPD Art. 35, caput, p.13); considers requirements, conditions and minimum safeguards (§1º); may request information and carry out inquiries (§2º); may designate certification bodies under its oversight (§3º) and review their acts (§4º); the safeguards also consider the technical and organizational measures of the processor, under Art. 46, §§1º and 2º (§5º) (LGPD Art. 35, §§1º-5º, p.13).
- `[from source]` Changes to the safeguards presented as sufficient are communicated to the ANPD (LGPD Art. 36, p.13).

## Practical use `[inference]`
- Map flows that cross borders: cloud, remote support, subprocessors and analytics. For each flow, record the Art. 33 case used and the evidence (clauses, certificate, highlighted consent).
- The consent in item VIII is an exception with its own requirements (prominence and prior information on the international nature); do not use the generic consent of Art. 8.
- `[extrapolation]` Standard clauses, the list of adequate countries and adaptation deadlines defined by the ANPD are regulation later than the compiled text and `[DATED]`: confirm the rule in force (see `extrapolacoes-e-datado.md`). NIST IR 8062 does not address international transfer (see M29 in `mapeamento-lgpd-nist.md`).
