Source: CL Appendix C, PDF p.395-396; Epilogue, PDF p.397.

## What Appendix C is
- A table that links each Ch. 17 code (C1 to T9) to the places in the book where the heuristic is cited, in chapter-page format (e.g. C1 at "16-276, 16-279, 17-292"). (CL p.395)
- It opens with a short sentence: a cross reference of "odores" (smells) and heuristics, followed by "Podem-se excluir todas as outras", a literal and confusing translation of the original ("all others may be removed"). (CL p.395)
- It lists exactly the 66 codes: C1-C5, E1-E2, F1-F4, G1-G36, J1-J3, N1-N7, T1-T9, in the same order as Ch. 17. (CL p.395-396)
- Ch. 17 announces the appendix "na página 409", but in this edition it is on printed pages 399-400 (PDF p.395-396). Do not use the cited number. (CL p.281, p.395-396)

## Epilogue
- In 2005, at an Agile conference in Denver, Elisabeth Hendrickson (spelled "Hedrickson" in the printed edition, checked on the image) gave the author a green wristband with the phrase "Obcecado por testes" (Test Obsessed). (CL p.397)
- The author has been obsessed with TDD since Kent Beck taught it to him, in 1999. (CL p.397)
- He cannot take off the wristband for a moral reason: it publicly declares his professional ethics and his commitment to writing the best code possible. (CL p.397)
- The wristband is a constant reminder of the promise to write clean code. (CL p.397)
- Reading for the agent [inference]: the book's closing is about discipline and professional commitment, not about more rules; it matches the Ch. 17 conclusion (clean code comes from a value system). (CL p.309, p.397)

## What these sections do NOT say / common confusions
- The appendix neither summarizes nor explains the heuristics; it only points to where they appear. For the content, use Ch. 17 (`cap17-odores-e-heuristicas.md`).
- The appendix is not a reliable page source in this edition; never cite "17-336" etc. as a page.
- The Epilogue brings no new rules and no list of practices.
- The Ch. 17 conclusion is incomplete in the PDF: p.309 ends mid-sentence and the next page is already Appendix A (printed pages 311-312 missing in the scan). Do not invent the ending. (CL p.309-310)
