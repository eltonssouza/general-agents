# 00 — Overview of what the captures say

Source: the five Overview captures (NG18-NG22). Everything below is a paraphrase of what the page presents; it is **marketing/high-level orientation**, not technical reference. Pagination in `INDEX.md` §1.

## What Angular is, according to the page
- A web framework for building fast, reliable applications, maintained by a dedicated team at Google; it offers tools, APIs and libraries, and promises to scale with team size and codebase size (NG18 p.1, NG19 p.2, NG20 p.1, NG21 p.1, NG22 p.1).
- The opening sentence of NG21 and NG22 adds that the applications are the ones "that users love" and says Angular.dev is the official home of the documentation (NG21 p.1, NG22 p.1).
- The page invites the reader to see code in **Essentials** (a quick look at what using Angular is like) or in the **Tutorial/Tutorials** (step by step) (NG18 p.1; NG21 p.1).

## Page structure (five sections, all versions)
1. **Features that drive development** — components, DI, signals, SSR/SSG/hydration, router, forms (and, in NG18-NG20, `@defer`).
2. **Develop faster** — CLI, DevTools, `ng update`, language service for the IDE.
3. **Ship with confidence** — verification in Google's monorepo, support policy and release schedule.
4. **Works at any scale** — i18n, security by default, Vite and esbuild, use in large Google apps.
5. **Open-source first / community** — GitHub, roadmap and RFCs, DevLibrary, GDEs, partnerships (Chrome's Aurora, Firebase, TensorFlow, Flutter, Material Design, Google Cloud).
Details and pages: `01-conceitos-por-tema.md`.
