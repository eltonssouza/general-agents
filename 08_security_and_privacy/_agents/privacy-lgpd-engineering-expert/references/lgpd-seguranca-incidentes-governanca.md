# LGPD: security, incidents, good practices and governance (Art. 46 to 51)

Pages = PDF (15-17). Labels: `[from source]`, `[inference]`, `[extrapolation]`, `[DATED]`. Not legal advice; refer to a lawyer/encarregado (DPO).

## Security and confidentiality (Section I)
- `[from source]` Processing agents must adopt technical and administrative security measures apt to protect the data from unauthorized access and from accidental or unlawful situations of destruction, loss, alteration, communication or any form of inadequate or unlawful processing (LGPD Art. 46, caput, p.15).
- `[from source]` The ANPD may rule on minimum technical standards, considering the nature of the information, the characteristics of the processing, the current state of technology (especially for sensitive data) and the principles of Art. 6º, caput (LGPD Art. 46, §1º, p.15).
- `[from source]` The measures must be observed "desde a fase de concepção do produto ou do serviço até a sua execução" (from the design phase of the product or service until its execution) (LGPD Art. 46, §2º, p.15). `[inference]` This is the textual anchor for what the market calls privacy by design / security by design; the expression itself does not appear in the law.
- `[from source]` The security obligation applies to the agents and to any person who intervenes in a phase of the processing, even after its end (LGPD Art. 47, p.15). Systems must be structured to meet security requirements, good practice and governance standards, general principles and regulatory rules (LGPD Art. 49, p.16).
- `[inference]` The law does not list controls (encryption, MFA etc.); the term "criptografia" (encryption) does not appear in the text. Concrete controls come from other sources (for example, the project's security collection, `[extrapolation]` with respect to these two sources). What the text gives is the criterion: apt measures, proportional to the nature of the data and to the state of technology.

## Security incidents (Art. 48)
- `[from source]` The controller must notify the ANPD and the data subject of the occurrence of a security incident that may cause relevant risk or harm to the data subjects (LGPD Art. 48, caput, p.16).
- `[from source]` Notification is made within a reasonable period, as defined by the ANPD, and mentions at least (LGPD Art. 48, §1º, I-VI, p.16): I nature of the personal data affected; II information on the data subjects involved; III technical and security measures used for protection, observing trade and industrial secrets; IV risks related to the incident; V reasons for delay, if not immediate; VI measures adopted or to be adopted to reverse or mitigate the effects.
- `[from source]` The ANPD verifies the severity and may order measures, such as wide dissemination of the fact in the media and measures to reverse or mitigate the effects (LGPD Art. 48, §2º, p.16). In judging severity, proof of adequate technical measures that make the data unintelligible to unauthorized third parties is assessed, within the technical limits of the services (LGPD Art. 48, §3º, p.16).
- `[from source]` The text does not fix a number of hours or days (the expression "72 horas" (72 hours) does not appear). The regulated deadline and the ANPD form are `[extrapolation]` and `[DATED]`: see `extrapolacoes-e-datado.md`.
- `[from source]` Connections: individual leaks or unauthorized accesses may be the subject of direct conciliation between controller and data subject; without agreement, the penalties apply (LGPD Art. 52, §7º, p.18). Demonstrated adoption of internal mechanisms to minimize damage and prompt adoption of corrective measures are sanction criteria (LGPD Art. 52, §1º, VIII and X, p.18).

## Good practices and governance (Section II)
- `[from source]` Controllers and processors, individually or through associations, may formulate rules of good practice and governance: organizational conditions, operating regime, procedures (including complaints and petitions from data subjects), security rules, technical standards, obligations of those involved, educational actions, internal supervision and risk mitigation mechanisms (LGPD Art. 50, caput, p.16). In establishing them, they consider the nature, scope, purpose and the probability and severity of the risks and benefits of the processing (LGPD Art. 50, §1º, p.16).
- `[from source]` Privacy governance program, in applying the principles of security and prevention (Art. 6º, VII and VIII), which at a minimum (LGPD Art. 50, §2º, I, p.16-17):
  - a) demonstrates the controller's commitment to internal processes and policies (LGPD Art. 50, §2º, I, a, p.16);
  - b) applies to the whole set of data under its control, regardless of how it was collected (LGPD Art. 50, §2º, I, b, p.16);
  - c) is adapted to the structure, scale, volume and sensitivity of the data; d) establishes policies and safeguards based on systematic assessment of privacy impacts and risks; e) aims at a relationship of trust with the data subject, with transparent action and mechanisms for data subject participation; f) is integrated into general governance, with internal and external oversight; g) includes incident response and remediation plans; h) is updated with continuous monitoring and periodic evaluations (LGPD Art. 50, §2º, I, c-h, p.17).
- `[from source]` The controller must demonstrate the effectiveness of the program when appropriate, especially at the request of the ANPD or of an entity that promotes good practices or codes of conduct (LGPD Art. 50, §2º, II, p.17). The good practice rules are published, periodically updated and may be recognized by the ANPD (LGPD Art. 50, §3º, p.17). The ANPD will encourage technical standards that facilitate control by data subjects (LGPD Art. 51, p.17).
