# LGPD: processing agents, record, RIPD, encarregado and civil liability (Art. 37 to 45)

Pages = PDF (14-15). Labels: `[from source]`, `[inference]`, `[extrapolation]`, `[DATED]`. Not legal advice; refer to a lawyer/encarregado (DPO).

## Controller and processor
- `[from source]` Definitions: the controller decides; the processor processes on behalf of the controller (LGPD Art. 5º, VI-VII, p.2-3). The processor processes according to the controller's instructions, and the controller verifies compliance with the instructions and the rules (LGPD Art. 39, p.14).
- `[from source]` Controller and processor keep a record of processing operations, especially when based on legitimate interest (LGPD Art. 37, p.14). The text does not enumerate the fields of the record; the ANPD may rule on the retention time of records and interoperability standards (LGPD Art. 40, p.14).
- `[inference]` Useful fields for the record (purpose, legal basis, categories of data and data subjects, sharing, international transfer, retention, security, processor) come from other articles; use `python -I scripts/ripd_templates.py registro`.

## Data protection impact report (RIPD, Relatório de impacto à proteção de dados pessoais)
- `[from source]` Definition: documentation describing the processing operations that may generate risks to civil liberties and fundamental rights, as well as measures, safeguards and risk mitigation mechanisms (LGPD Art. 5º, XVII, p.3).
- `[from source]` The ANPD may order the controller to prepare a RIPD, including for sensitive data, regarding its operations, under regulation, with trade and industrial secrets observed (LGPD Art. 38, caput, p.14). Minimum content: description of the types of data collected; methodology used for collection and for ensuring information security; the controller's analysis of the measures, safeguards and risk mitigation mechanisms adopted (LGPD Art. 38, sole paragraph, p.14).
- `[from source]` Other mentions: the ANPD may request a RIPD when the basis is legitimate interest (LGPD Art. 10, §3º, p.6); in the exceptions of Art. 4º, III (LGPD Art. 4º, §3º, p.2); it may ask Public Authorities to publish a RIPD (LGPD Art. 32, p.12); and it issues regulations on RIPD for cases of high risk to the principles (LGPD Art. 55-J, XIII, p.21).
- `[inference]` The text does not say when a RIPD is "mandatory" on the controller's own initiative; it appears as an order or request from the ANPD. As good practice, governance programs include systematic assessment of impacts and risks (LGPD Art. 50, §2º, I, d, p.17). The RIPD template is in `scripts/ripd_templates.py ripd`, separating minimum content (from the source) from additions (inference).

## Encarregado
- `[from source]` The controller must appoint an encarregado (LGPD Art. 41, caput, p.14). Identity and contact are publicly disclosed, clearly and objectively, preferably on the controller's website (LGPD Art. 41, §1º, p.14).
- `[from source]` Activities: I accept complaints and communications from data subjects, provide clarifications and take action; II receive communications from the ANPD and take action; III guide employees and contractors on data protection practices; IV other duties set by the controller or by complementary rules (LGPD Art. 41, §2º, I-IV, p.14).
- `[from source]` The ANPD may establish complementary rules on the definition and duties of the encarregado, including cases of exemption depending on the nature and size of the entity or the volume of operations (LGPD Art. 41, §3º, p.14). §4º appears as vetoed (LGPD Art. 41, §4º, p.14).
- `[from source]` Art. 5º, VIII (p.3), in the wording given by Law 15.352/2026 according to the compiled text, describes the encarregado as appointed by the controller and the processor. `[DATED]`/check: the caput of Art. 41 still assigns the appointment to the controller; verify the current wording with a lawyer.
- `[extrapolation]` Qualification of the encarregado, the possibility of a legal entity and conflict-of-interest rules come from ANPD regulation (not in the collection).

## Civil liability (Art. 42-45)
- `[from source]` Whoever, when processing data, causes property, moral, individual or collective damage in violation of data protection legislation is obliged to repair it (LGPD Art. 42, caput, p.14).
- `[from source]` To ensure compensation: the processor is jointly liable when it breaches the legislation or does not follow the controller's lawful instructions, being equated with the controller; controllers directly involved are jointly liable (LGPD Art. 42, §1º, I-II, p.14-15). The judge may reverse the burden of proof in favor of the data subject (plausibility, vulnerability or excessively burdensome proof) (LGPD Art. 42, §2º, p.15). Collective actions are possible (LGPD Art. 42, §3º, p.15). There is a right of recourse in proportion to participation in the damage (LGPD Art. 42, §4º, p.15).
- `[from source]` Exclusions: proving that it did not carry out the attributed processing; that there was no violation of the legislation; or that the damage results from the exclusive fault of the data subject or a third party (LGPD Art. 43, I-III, p.15).
- `[from source]` Irregular processing: does not comply with the legislation or does not provide the security the data subject may expect, considering the manner of performance, the result and the risks reasonably expected and the techniques available at the time; whoever failed to adopt the measures of Art. 46 answers for the damages of the security breach (LGPD Art. 44, I-III and sole paragraph, p.15). Consumer relations follow the relevant legislation (LGPD Art. 45, p.15).
- `[inference]` For engineering: processing contracts should record lawful instructions and responsibilities, and the controller needs evidence that it supervises the processor (Art. 39). Art. 44 ties "the security the data subject may expect" to the state of the art at the time: document security decisions with date and reason. Connection (only inferred) with NIST predictability (M20 in `mapeamento-lgpd-nist.md`).
