# Fidelity report: system-design-microservices-expert (v2, post-audit)

This report replaces the previous one, which declared "19/19 PASS, 0 hallucinations, independent audit" but only validated page ranges and did not check the claims against the pages. Here is what was found, what was corrected, what was not audited, and what was NOT done.

## 1. Scope, method, and convention

- Sources: 11 PDFs in `03_design_and_architecture/`. Text extracted with PyMuPDF (`tests/extract_sources.py`) into `SIGLA.txt` with `=== PAGE N ===` markers; N = PDF page. Three tables/figures as images were viewed as images: SDH p.7 (nines), SAP p.53 (arrows), and JMG p.21 (HTTP table).
- Adopted convention: PDF page; offset map in `references/INDEX.md` (SAP N-7, SOA N-10, MS-MIC N-15, MS-CLD N-10, MS-WEB N-9, EDS N-15, JMG N-15, REST N-9; SDH/SBC/APIGW N). Package v1 mixed PDF pages and printed numbers without saying so (e.g., "SAP p.8-14" in PDF, but "SAP p.23-40" in printed pages; "MS-MIC p.25-35" printed).
- Backup of the original package (v1) and the old tests: `scratch/audit/system-design-microservices-expert/v1_backup*`.
- No subagents: sequential work, notes in `scratch/.../notes/`.

### 3.2 Manual (reading the cited pages; single reviewer)
Sample of 90 claims covering all 14 reference files, prompt, numbers, "always/never", comparisons, tables, and pattern names. Result:

| Class | Items | % |

Relevant examples (v1 -> what the pages say):
1. **Saga, pivot, and Outbox "from MS-MIC p.210-221"**: those pages are Value Objects. Saga is in MS-CLD p.102-103 and JMG p.63; the Outbox in MS-MIC p.73, p.158-160; the "pivot/compensable/retryable" classification does not exist in the corpus.
2. **Circuit Breaker with 3 states, "5 failures or 50% in 10 s" and Half-Open (MS-MIC p.250-254)**: the text describes opening after failures, failing fast, and allowing a trial call; the real example is 5 consecutive failures and 30 s (MS-MIC p.322) or 100 failures and a 30 s CheckCircuit (MS-CLD p.127); the term Half-Open does not exist.
3. **"Richards stresses that 2PC violates autonomy (SAP p.32)"**: SAP p.40 talks about a "transaction compensation framework"; it also allows a shared database between services, the opposite of the database-per-service that the package attributed to the book.
4. **SAP 1-5 star matrix (p.47-51)**: the SAP uses Low/High (arrow figure on p.53); microkernel scalability is Low (v1 said Medium 3) and EDA development is Low (v1: Medium 3). The "80/20 rule" was also distorted (SAP p.14: 20% pass-through is typical; if inverted, consider open layers).
5. **gRPC "7 to 10 times faster, 60-80% smaller (MS-CLD p.52)"**: MS-CLD p.91 says "up to 8x" and 60-80%; p.52 is something else.
6. **Table of safe/idempotent methods and Richardson Maturity Model "REST p.11-19"**: the Spring tutorial does not include them; the table is in JMG p.21-22 and the RMM is only a link in MS-MIC p.68.
7. **Little's Law, latency tail, PACELC and 4 classes "SDH p.8-9"; range/hash/directory sharding "SDH p.20"; B-Tree vs LSM, XFetch, vnodes**: none appear in the SDH.
8. **Starbucks durations (10 s / 2 min)**, Kafka 7-day retention (EDS p.142: two weeks), "tombstone" (EDS uses "null value/delete marker"), "OpenTelemetry/TraceContext in the MS-CLD" (only JMG p.141 and p.152, as drafts).

- **References (14 files rewritten, names preserved)**: each claim was reread on the PDF page; 826 page citations in the references; labels: 505 `[from source]`, 24 `[inference]`, 64 `[extrapolation]`, 23 `[DATED]`. - **Scripts rewritten with declared origin**: `sysdesign_lookup.py` (curated index of 69 concepts, 295 terms verified against the pages, explicit `NOT COVERED`), `analyze_tradeoffs.py` (SDH p.7 table of nines; `[extrapolation]` formulas; no product classification), `audit_saga.py` (rules S1-S8 with origin per finding; pivot `[extrapolation]`), `calc_rate_limit.py` (backoff reproduces the books' examples; token bucket `[extrapolation]`; CB by simulated time; no invented headers).
