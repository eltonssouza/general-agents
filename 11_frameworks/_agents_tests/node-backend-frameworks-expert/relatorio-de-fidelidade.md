- `[extrapolation]` (declared as such and **outside** the sources): `req.locals` vs `res.locals` (EXPHB p.18); `app.configure`/`express.errorHandler` nonexistent in current versions (NODEHB p.199); Axios vs. Fetch comparisons may be outdated (NODEHB p.137-138). These labels appear in the references and in the linter messages.
- Third-party claims preserved as "according to the text" and not validated: "two mandatory headers" (NODEHB p.131); 2-3x serialization factor (FSTF p.12); SWC "20x" (NEST p.5); "very performant" performance (EXPHB p.2).
- Errors in the texts themselves are listed in `references/casos-e-antipadroes.md` (A1-A12); the agent warns before copying examples.
- The linter is **heuristic** (regex + brace counting); it has expected false positives and negatives. Each rule carries origin and page; `--rules` lists all of them (35).
