- Series described: **LLaMA** (LLaMA→2→3→3.1; 3.1 with up to 405B, 15T tokens, 128K context; LLaMA 65B trained on 2,048 A100-80G; 3.1-405B on more than 16,000 H100) (p.11); **Mistral** (7B, NeMo 12B, Large 2 123B, Mixtral SMoE 8×7B and 8×22B) (p.11); **Gemma** (Gemma-2 2B/9B/27B with 2T/8T/13T tokens) (p.11); **Qwen** (up to Qwen2.5, with up to 18T tokens) (p.11); **GLM** (GLM-4 up to 9B) (p.11); **Baichuan** (7B/13B; Baichuan-2 with 2.6T tokens) (p.11).
- LLaMA derivative tree (Fig. 5, p.12): Stanford Alpaca (LLaMA 7B, 52K demonstrations generated by self-instruct with text-davinci-003), Alpaca-LoRA, Vicuna (ShareGPT conversations), Koala, BELLE; Vicuna is preferred in multimodal LLMs (LLaVA, MiniGPT-4, InstructBLIP, PandaGPT) (p.12).
- OpenAI APIs then current: ada/babbage/curie/davinci (babbage=1B, curie=6.7B, davinci=175B), Codex families, GPT-3.5 (gpt-3.5-turbo) and GPT-4 (gpt-4, gpt-4-32k, gpt-4-1106-preview, gpt-4-vision-preview) (p.12-13).
- Tab. 1 (p.8) summarizes LLMs >10B with date, size, data, hardware and time (e.g., PaLM 540B, 780B tokens, 6,144 TPU v4; Chinchilla 70B, 1.4T tokens; Gopher 280B, 300B tokens, 4,096 TPU v3, 920 h).

## 4.1 Pre-training data (LLMS p.16-21)
- General sources (web, conversations, books) vs specialized (multilingual, scientific, code) (p.17-18). PaLM uses 122 languages and BLOOM 46 (p.17). Excess dialogue can cause declarative instructions to be treated as the start of a conversation (p.17). Training on code may be a source of complex reasoning/CoT (speculation) (p.18).
- **Preprocessing (Fig. 7, p.19)**: filtering (classifier vs heuristics: language, metric/perplexity, statistics, keywords; classifiers may remove dialects) (p.18); **deduplication** at sentence/document/set level and prevention of train/evaluation contamination (p.18); PII reduction (p.18-19); **tokenization** (BPE, byte-level BPE, WordPiece, Unigram, SentencePiece) (p.19).
- Effect of quality: duplicates cause "double descent", can destabilize and worsen the ability to copy from context (impacts ICL) (p.20).
- **Data scheduling (p.20-21)**: mixture (LLaMA: >80% web, 6.5% code, 4.5% books, 2.5% arXiv; Falcon web only; DoReMi optimizes domain weights with small models) and curriculum (CodeLLaMA: 2T general → 500B code → 100B Python; Llemma: math with 5% general data; long context: 2.5T@4K → 20B@16K) (p.20-21).
- Summary (p.21): diverse collection, cleaning (dedup, quality/toxicity/privacy), tokenizer trained on the filtered corpus, choose mixture/curriculum by training small models and monitoring checkpoints.

## Rules
1. "Which data to use?": diversify sources and increase the share of the target skill (LLMS p.20-21).
2. Quality before volume: dedup + filter (LLMS p.20).
